Senior Data Analyst — Growth & Retention

About the Role
We are looking for a Senior Data Analyst to join our Growth & Retention team. You will work closely with product managers, engineers, and marketing to drive data-informed decisions that improve user retention and accelerate revenue growth.

Responsibilities
- Design and maintain dashboards and reports that track key business metrics including churn, retention, and lifetime value
- Conduct deep-dive analyses to identify drivers of user churn and recommend actionable interventions
- Build predictive models to forecast retention rates and identify at-risk users
- Design and analyze A/B tests to evaluate product changes and marketing campaigns
- Partner with the data engineering team to ensure clean, reliable data pipelines
- Present findings to senior leadership with clear, compelling data storytelling
- Define and track KPIs for Growth and Retention initiatives

Required Skills & Qualifications
- 4+ years of experience in data analysis or analytics engineering
- Advanced SQL skills — comfortable with complex joins, window functions, CTEs
- Proficiency in Python for data manipulation (pandas, NumPy) and modeling (scikit-learn, XGBoost)
- Experience with data visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI, or Looker)
- Hands-on experience with A/B testing, statistical hypothesis testing, and experiment design
- Familiarity with machine learning concepts — classification, regression, churn prediction
- Experience with cloud data platforms (Snowflake, BigQuery, or Redshift)
- Strong communication skills and ability to translate technical findings for non-technical stakeholders

Nice to Have
- Experience with Spark or distributed data processing
- Familiarity with dbt for data transformation
- Knowledge of customer segmentation and cohort analysis techniques
- Experience in a SaaS or subscription business context
